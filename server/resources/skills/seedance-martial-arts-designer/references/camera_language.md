# Camera Language Reference

## Shot Types

| Shot Type | Chinese | When to Use | Seedance Keywords |
|-----------|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| Extreme wide | 大远景 | Establish scale, isolation | extreme wide shot, 大远景 |
| Wide | 全景 | Show full environment and action | wide shot, 全景 |
| Medium | 中景 | Show character + immediate action | medium shot, 中景 |
| Medium-close | 中近景 | Upper body action detail | medium close-up, 中近景 |
| Close-up | 特写 | Impact moments, facial expressions | close-up, 特写 |
| Extreme close-up | 大特写 | Weapon detail, eye contact | extreme close-up, 大特写 |

## Camera Movements

| Movement | Chinese | When to Use | Seedance Keywords |
|----------|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| Tracking | 跟拍 | Chase, moving combat | tracking shot, 跟拍镜头 |
| Orbiting | 环绕 | Standoff, environment showcase | orbiting shot, 环绕镜头 |
| Push in | 推进 | Build tension, focus attention | push in, 缓慢推进 |
| Pull out | 拉远 | Reveal context, aftermath | pull out, 拉远 |
| Crane up | 升起 | Reveal scale, vertical movement | crane shot, 升起镜头 |
| Tilt down | 俯摇 | Reveal from top to bottom | tilt down, 俯摇 |
| Handheld | 手持 | Chaos, urgency, immersion | handheld, 手持跟拍 |
| Dolly | 移动 | Lateral movement alongside action | dolly shot, 移动镜头 |

## Focal Length Guide

| Focal Length | Effect | Best For |
|--------------|--------|----------|
| 24mm (wide) | Expansive, immersive | Temple halls, rooftops, environment |
| 35mm (standard wide) | Natural perspective, versatile | General action, chase scenes |
| 50mm (normal) | Natural, documentary feel | Indoor fights, tavern brawls |
| 85mm (short tele) | Compressed, intimate | Sword duels, character moments |
| 135mm+ (tele) | Extreme compression | Projectile tracking, distant action |

## Frame Rate Recommendations

| Frame Rate | Effect | Best For |
|------------|--------|----------|
| 24fps | Cinematic, film-like | Poetic scenes, dramatic moments |
| 30fps | Standard, smooth | General action, most scenes |
| 120fps slow-mo | Detailed impact | Collisions, projectile flight |
| 240fps super slow | Ultra detail | Critical strike moments |

## Shot List Format

Output camera work as a 4-segment shot list:

```
Shot 1 (0-1s): [Description]
  Movement: [type] | Angle: [type] | Focal: [mm] | Speed: [fast/slow]

Shot 2 (1-2s): [Description]
  Movement: [type] | Angle: [type] | Focal: [mm] | Speed: [fast/slow]

Shot 3 (2-3s): [Description]
  Movement: [type] | Angle: [type] | Focal: [mm] | Speed: [fast/slow]

Shot 4 (3-5s): [Description]
  Movement: [type] | Angle: [type] | Focal: [mm] | Speed: [fast/slow]
```

## Aspect Ratio Guide

| Ratio | Best For | Effect |
|------|----------|--------|
| 16:9 | General purpose, tavern, bamboo | Standard cinematic |
| 21:9 | Modern action, rooftop | Wider tension, horizontal space |
| 2.35:1 | Epic, poetic, temple, snowfield | Ultra-wide, cinematic grandeur |
| 9:16 | Mobile-first content | Vertical immersion |
